Common local problems

What we hear about most in Nampa

Irrigation-raised groundwater

Nampa & Meridian Irrigation District and Pioneer Irrigation District laterals crisscross the city. Summer flows can saturate soils around older crawlspaces.

Flood-irrigated yards

Some older lots still flood-irrigate. If the grade slopes toward the house, that water ends up against the foundation every turn.

Attic condensation in new builds

Airtight construction plus long inversions means warm, moist air finds its way into attics and condenses on cold roof sheathing.

FAQs

Nampa mold questions

Why is my Nampa crawlspace wet only in summer?

That pattern almost always tracks irrigation season. Water delivered through canals and laterals from spring through fall raises shallow groundwater and saturates soils near the foundation. Once deliveries stop in October, it dries.

Can my irrigation district help with water near my foundation?

Districts maintain their canals and main laterals, not private ditches or yard grading. If you suspect a district facility is leaking, contact the district directly; Nampa & Meridian Irrigation District and Pioneer Irrigation District both serve parts of the city.

Is mold in a new-construction Nampa home covered by the builder?

Many builders offer a one-year workmanship warranty and longer structural coverage. Moisture from a construction defect may fall under it; document the problem and contact the builder promptly in writing.

Who handles building permits for repairs in Nampa?

The City of Nampa Building Safety division issues permits for rebuild work after remediation, such as structural or plumbing repairs.
