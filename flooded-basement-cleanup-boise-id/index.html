Flooded Basement Cleanup in Boise, ID

A flooded basement is a pump-out, a salvage job and a drying job all at once. What can be saved depends on where the water came from and how fast it’s removed.

How it works

The basement cleanup sequence

01
Before anyone goes down

Power off, source identified

Never walk into water that may be touching outlets, a furnace or a water heater. Power is shut off at the panel by someone standing on dry ground.

02
Hour 1–3

Pump-out

Submersible pumps remove deep water; extraction tools follow for carpet and the last inch on the slab.

03
Day 1

Contents & salvage

Furniture, boxes and stored items moved out and sorted. Anything that was in contaminated water and can’t be disinfected is discarded.

04
Day 1–2

Tear-out

Carpet pad, lower drywall and wet insulation removed so the slab and framing can dry.

05
Day 2–5

Drying & mold prevention

Dehumidifiers run until the slab and studs read dry; antimicrobial applied where needed per IICRC S500.

Causes

Why Boise-area basements flood

Spring

Snowmelt & rain-on-snow

Fast thaws send water off frozen yards straight toward window wells and foundations with poor grading.

Summer

High water table

Irrigation season raises groundwater near canals; a failed sump pump lets it rise through the slab.

Winter

Burst lines indoors

Frozen pipes in basement rim joists or a failed water heater are the most common indoor causes.

Prevention tips

Keep the next one from happening

Test your sump pump each springPour a bucket of water into the pit and confirm it starts and discharges well away from the house.

Add window-well coversBasement window wells fill fast in a spring storm; covers and a gravel drain help.

Extend downspouts 6+ feetShort downspouts dump roof water right against the foundation.

Store items on shelves, not the floorPlastic bins on metal shelving survive a small flood; cardboard boxes don’t.

FAQs

Flooded basement FAQs

Is basement flooding covered by homeowners insurance?

Flooding from a burst pipe inside the house usually is. Groundwater or surface water entering from outside generally isn’t covered without flood insurance, and sump pump failure needs a specific endorsement. FEMA’s FloodSmart explains the difference.

Can I pump out my basement myself?

Yes with a rented pump, once power is safely off. If water is very deep and outside soil is saturated, pump it down gradually — removing it too fast can let outside pressure crack walls.

Can basement carpet be saved after a flood?

If the water was clean and is removed within a day or two, carpet can sometimes be dried and re-laid, but pad is nearly always replaced. Carpet touched by groundwater or sewage is discarded.

How long does a flooded basement take to dry?

Typically three to five days with professional dehumidifiers after tear-out. Concrete slabs and block walls can take longer than wood framing.
