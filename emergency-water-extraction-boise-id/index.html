Causes

Where Treasure Valley water emergencies come from

January

Frozen & split pipes

Single-digit cold snaps freeze lines in exterior walls, garages and crawlspaces. The leak usually shows when it thaws, not when it freezes.

Any time

Failed water heaters

Tanks in garages and closets often fail at the 10–12 year mark, releasing 40–50 gallons plus whatever the supply keeps feeding.

Appliances

Supply hoses

Rubber washing-machine and ice-maker lines burst without warning. Braided steel hoses are a cheap upgrade.

Prevention tips

Lower the odds of a 2 a.m. flood

Know where your main shutoff isFind it, label it, and make sure it turns before you need it.

Disconnect hoses before first frostA hose left on a frost-free spigot can still split the pipe inside the wall.

Open sink cabinets in deep coldLets warm air reach pipes on exterior walls; a slow drip helps too.

Add a leak sensor or auto-shutoffCheap sensors under the water heater and washer catch slow leaks early.

FAQs

Water extraction FAQs

Can I use a shop vac instead of calling for extraction?

For a small spill on hard flooring, yes. For water that soaked into carpet and pad or reached the walls, a shop vac removes only surface water. The trapped moisture is what leads to odor, delamination and mold.

Should I pull up my wet carpet myself?

Wet carpet is heavy and the tack strip is sharp. Pulling it back can help drying, but the pad usually needs replacing anyway. Photograph everything first for insurance before removing anything.

How long before water damage turns into mold?

The EPA says growth can start within 24 to 48 hours on damp materials, which is why the first day matters most.

Will my insurance pay for water extraction?

Sudden events like a burst pipe or failed water heater are typically covered; gradual leaks and flooding from outside water usually aren’t. Report the loss promptly — most Idaho policies require timely notice and “reasonable steps” to limit damage.

Is it safe to stay in the house while it dries?

Usually, as long as the water was clean and electrical areas are safe. Expect noise from air movers and warmer rooms from dehumidifiers for several days.
