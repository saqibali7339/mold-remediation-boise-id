Category 3 water — don’t wait it out

Sewage Backup Cleanup in Boise, ID

Water coming up through a floor drain, basement toilet or shower is classified as “black water.” It carries bacteria and viruses, soaks into everything porous, and isn’t something to mop up with a shop vac.

Right now, before anyone arrives:

  • Stop running water anywhere in the house — every drain feeds the same line.
  • Keep kids and pets out of the affected area.
  • If water is near outlets or the furnace, shut power at the panel only if you can reach it dry.
  • Take photos for insurance before anything is moved.

Health & property risks

Why sewage is handled differently

HealthWhat’s in it

  • Bacteria such as E. coli, plus viruses and parasites, per CDC sanitation guidance.
  • Gases like hydrogen sulfide in enclosed basements.
  • Secondary mold growth once the area sits damp past 48 hours.

PropertyWhat it ruins

  • Carpet and pad can’t be safely cleaned after Category 3 contact — they’re removed.
  • Drywall wicks contaminated water upward; it’s cut out above the waterline.
  • Particleboard cabinets and furniture swell and usually can’t be saved.
Standards

How the industry classifies water

The IICRC S500 standard sorts water by contamination level. Sewage is always the highest category.

1

Clean water

Broken supply line, overflowing sink. Can degrade to Cat 2 within ~48 hours.

2

Grey water

Washer or dishwasher discharge, toilet overflow with urine only.

3

Black water

Sewage backup, toilet with feces, outside floodwater. Full PPE, removal of porous materials, antimicrobial treatment.

FAQs

Sewage backup questions

Who pays if the city sewer backs up into my basement?

It depends whether the blockage was in the city main or your private service lateral. Boise residents can report a suspected main backup to Public Works; the lateral from your house to the main is generally the owner’s responsibility.

Does homeowners insurance cover sewage backup?

Standard policies usually exclude it. Coverage typically comes from a “water backup and sump overflow” endorsement. The Idaho Dept. of Insurance recommends confirming this before a loss.

Can I clean up a small sewage backup myself?

A few cups on tile from a toilet overflow, cleaned immediately with disinfectant, is manageable. Anything that reached carpet, drywall or a finished basement should be handled with the full Category 3 protocol.

Why did my basement floor drain back up?

Common causes are tree roots in older clay laterals, grease buildup, or a collapsed pipe. In older Boise neighborhoods, original clay or cast-iron sewer lines are a frequent culprit.
