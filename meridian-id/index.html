Local issues

Moisture patterns in Meridian homes

Yearround

Bath fans that vent into the attic

A common shortcut in fast-built subdivisions: exhaust ducts that end in the insulation instead of at a roof cap. Winter attic sheathing turns dark above bathrooms.

Apr–Oct

Crawlspaces on old farm ground

Many subdivisions sit on land that was flood-irrigated for decades. Ten Mile and Five Mile Creek drains and Nampa & Meridian Irrigation District laterals keep groundwater higher in summer.

Dec–Feb

Window condensation in tight homes

Airtight builds hold cooking and shower humidity. During inversions, window frames and closet corners on north walls stay wet.

Rebuild permits go through City of Meridian Building Services.

FAQs

Meridian mold questions

Why is there mold in my attic if my Meridian house is only 10 years old?

The usual culprits are bathroom fans that vent into the attic instead of outside, or blocked soffit vents from blown insulation. Both put warm, wet air onto cold roof sheathing all winter.

Will my HOA or builder pay for mold in a new Meridian home?

HOAs typically cover common areas only. Builder warranties vary — a construction defect like an unvented fan may be covered within the warranty window. Document it in writing to the builder.

Is my crawlspace supposed to have standing water in summer?

No. Some dampness in irrigation season is common on old farm ground, but standing water means grading, gutters or the vapor barrier need attention — and joists should be checked for growth.

Does Meridian require a permit for mold removal?

Removal itself usually doesn’t. Structural repairs, new electrical or plumbing, and some rebuilds do; Meridian Building Services can confirm.
