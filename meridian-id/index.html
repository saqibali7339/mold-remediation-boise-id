Why it happens here

Why this happens in Meridian

Four local patterns behind most Meridian calls.

1

Sealed-tight new construction

Growth-boom homes prioritize energy efficiency. Without steady ventilation, moisture from showers, cooking, and laundry condenses on the coldest surfaces: inside closets and behind furniture on exterior walls.

2

A long, wet irrigation season

Canal water runs spring into fall, keeping soil around many Meridian foundations damp for months. That ground moisture wicks into slab edges and basement walls, feeding slow growth nobody notices until the smell arrives.

3

Fast-moving home sales

Fresh paint covers a lot in a fast-moving market. A ceiling stain or warped baseboard in a new purchase often traces to a leak the previous owner patched instead of drying.

4

Hard freezes on exposed plumbing

January cold snaps punish supply lines in garages, crawlspaces, and exterior walls. A pipe that splits overnight can soak a wall cavity for hours before anyone sees water, and mold can follow within days. The EPA advises fixing the moisture and cleaning the mold promptly, whatever the type (EPA — Mold, Moisture & Your Home).

Sources: EPA — Mold, Moisture & Your Home CDC — Mold and Health

Questions

Meridian questions, answered

Do you cover my Meridian neighborhood?

All of them: Paramount, Bridgetower, Tuscany, newer builds toward Ten Mile, and older pockets near downtown. Inside city limits or just outside, call (321) 985-3339 and we will confirm.

Can a newer home really get mold?

Yes. Age does not protect a house; moisture does the damage. Tightly built newer homes trap humidity well, and growth shows up in homes barely a year old when ventilation or drainage is off. The CDC notes that damp indoor spaces can irritate eyes, nose, throat, and lungs, so it is worth checking early (CDC — Mold and Health).

How fast can someone come out?

Call (321) 985-3339 and tell us what is happening. Active water gets priority routing; smells and stains take the next inspection slot. You will get a straight answer on timing before you hang up.

Will insurance cover this?

It depends on your policy and the cause: sudden pipe bursts are treated differently than slow leaks. We document everything with photos and moisture readings for your claim, but the coverage decision sits with your insurer.

What should I do while I wait?

If water is flowing, shut off the main valve and move valuables off wet floors. Do not run the HVAC if you suspect mold in the ducts. Ventilate the affected room if you safely can.
