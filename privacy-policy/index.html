Privacy Policy

Plain-English summary of what we collect when you use this site, and what we do with it. Last updated October 2026.

Information we collect

This website has no contact forms, so we do not collect information through web forms. When you call (321) 985-3339, we receive your phone number and whatever details you choose to share about your mold or water damage situation so we can help.

We also collect basic, anonymous website usage data (such as pages visited and device type) through standard analytics tools. This helps us understand which pages are useful. It does not identify you personally.

How we use it

We use the information from your call for one purpose: connecting you with an independent local mold or water damage provider in the Treasure Valley who can inspect, quote, or perform the work you need.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your phone number for marketing calls or text campaigns.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses basic cookies and analytics to measure traffic and improve pages. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, though some features may behave differently.

Third parties

When you ask for help, we may share the details of your call (your phone number and a description of the problem) with the local service provider who will handle your job. That provider has its own privacy practices for anything it collects from you directly.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect against fraud or misuse of this site.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.
