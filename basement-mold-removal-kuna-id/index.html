Below-grade growth

Basement Mold Removal in Kuna, ID

Kuna basements sit over hardpan that refuses to absorb water — so runoff, well-room seeps, and humid crawlspace air all end up feeding growth on below-grade walls. We remove the mold and correct the moisture path that caused it.

Prevention

Keeping Kuna basements dry

Extend downspouts at least six feet from the foundationOn hardpan, water dumped at the foundation line travels sideways along the caliche and presses straight into basement walls.

Check well-room fittings twice a yearPressure tanks, softeners, and filters add a dozen joints inside the home. A five-minute look catches the drip before it becomes a wall.

Keep stored items off basement floorsBoxes on concrete wick moisture and hide the first signs of seepage. Shelving buys you visibility.

Run a dehumidifier through irrigation seasonApril to October is when Kuna's groundwater peaks. Holding the basement under 50% humidity starves mold of its moisture.

Sources

How water reaches Kuna basements

01

Hardpan lateral flow

Caliche sits shallow under much of Kuna. Water from roofs and sprinklers hits that layer and moves sideways, arriving at foundation walls instead of draining down.

02

Well equipment rooms

Pressure tanks, softeners, and filtration add joints and valves inside the home. A slow drip at a tank fitting mists the surrounding wall for months before anyone notices.

03

Crawlspace-to-basement air

Partial basements joined to crawlspaces share air. A damp crawlspace on a former field parcel loads the whole below-grade space with humidity every summer.

A basement job, described

Typical scenario (illustrative example — not a past job)

A family in a 1990s Kuna home notices a damp smell every August. The inspection finds the downspouts dumping two feet from the foundation onto hardpan, with water tracking along the caliche straight to the basement wall. Mold covers the lower two feet of drywall behind storage boxes. The crew removes the affected board, HEPA-cleans the framing, and the downspouts get extended ten feet out — the actual fix.

Our checklist

What removal includes

  • Moisture mapping of all below-grade walls to find the true wet boundary
  • Containment of the work area with HEPA-filtered negative air
  • Removal of mold-bearing drywall, insulation, and baseboards to clean margins
  • HEPA vacuuming and cleaning of framing, masonry, and slab
  • Identification and correction plan for the water source
  • Documented dry readings before the space is closed back up
